About Kandy Achievers

A court built for real progress

Kandy Achievers Badminton Academy trains players in Kandy who want to get serious about the game. Juniors chasing their first tournament, adults chasing a cleaner smash, anyone ready to put in the work.

Every session is coached, every drill has a purpose, and every player leaves sharper than they walked in. No filler, no wasted court time.
